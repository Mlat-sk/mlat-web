About us

Traditional craft, modern technology

Mlat s.r.o. specialises in hardwood processing – oak, beech, ash, walnut and other species depending on current availability. We supply oak, beech, ash and walnut timber, boards and beams. We process logs in our own facility in Málinec, from the first cut through drying to final finishing.

Nothing goes to waste: offcuts become edge-glued panel stock, and sawdust and wood dust are pressed into binder-free wood briquettes.

The company draws on more than 20 years of experience in wood production – from selecting the logs to the finished product. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern machinery.

Hardwood products

Production

From raw log to finished product

The whole production process takes place at our facility in Málinec. Follow it step by step – most steps come with a video.

01

Unloading and sorting logs

Logs are delivered and sorted by length as they are unloaded.

02

Loading onto the conveyor ramp

Logs are loaded onto the conveyor ramp, which feeds them to the band saw.

03

Sawing the logs

Horizontal band saw MEBOR HTZ 1100 PRO

Fast and precise blade positioning. The saw splits logs up to 1,000 mm in diameter. We bought the saw with the support of EU funds.

About our EU-supported project →

04

Boards ready for the customer or for drying

Freshly sawn boards go straight to the customer, or we prepare them for kiln drying – as required.

05

Drying in the hot-air kiln

Loading the kiln and drying the timber at controlled temperature, humidity and steam level.

06

Ripping boards lengthwise

Multi-blade rip saw Raimann WENIG ProfiRip 310 Opti

A universal multi-blade rip saw for cutting timber lengthwise.

07

Planing and profiling

Four-sided planer-moulder GRIGGIO 240

Preparing beams and material for edge-glued panels.

08

Making wood briquettes

Briquetting press Briklis 100

We press sawdust and wood dust from our production into binder-free wood briquettes.
